# Credits and influences

An informal, running list of what inspired this project. Inspiration, not duplication: we borrow colors and moods, never logos, characters or artwork.

- **Monument Valley**, the game series by ustwo games, first released in 2014. The flat, minimal look: say the idea of a thing with as few lines as possible. https://www.monumentvalleygame.com/mv3
- **The Australian Open**, by way of a 2013 tournament towel. The palette: navy, court blue and ball green.
- **Typefaces on Google Fonts**: Roboto (Christian Robertson, with ParaType and Font Bureau), Roboto Mono (Christian Robertson), Lato (Łukasz Dziedzic), Lora (Cyreal), Oswald (Vernon Adams, Kalapi Gajjar and Cyreal) and Ubuntu (Dalton Maag). Starting points for the lettering of the name; the first version uses Roboto Mono.
