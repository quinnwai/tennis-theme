credits

What inspired this page. Inspiration, not duplication: it borrows colors and moods, never logos, characters or artwork.

  • Monument Valley, the game series by ustwo games, first released in 2014. The flat, minimal look: say the idea of a thing with as few lines as possible.
  • The Australian Open, by way of a 2013 tournament towel. The palette: navy, court blue and ball green.
  • Ubuntu by Dalton Maag, from Google Fonts. The lettering of the name. Roboto, Roboto Mono, Lato, Lora and Oswald were the other starting points.
